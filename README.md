# Project Capable

An AI skill file that teaches disaster and emergency preparedness from zero to top-1% ready.

Give the file to your AI assistant and it becomes a tutor. It walks you through 14 domains with interactive lessons, Socratic questioning, and a readiness checklist, at your pace.

## How to use it

**Claude:** add `disaster-preparedness-tutor/SKILL.md` as a skill, then say "teach me preparedness".

**Any other AI assistant:** paste the contents of `SKILL.md` into a new conversation and say "teach me preparedness".

At the end of each session, say "save state" and keep the block it gives you. Start the next session with "resume from:" followed by that block.

## What it covers

| Layer | Domains |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Mindset and decision-making; risk assessment and planning |
| Core capabilities | Medical; sheltering in place; evacuation; field survival; communications; security |
| Scenario playbooks | Common emergencies; active shooter; regional disasters; rare high-consequence events |
| Sustainment | Community and leadership; practice, maintenance, recovery |

## How it works

- **First five sessions:** each ends with one thing done that day.
- **Every module:** a short review, an interactive lesson, questions one at a time, then a two-question quiz.
- **Three stages:** ready for 72 hours, then 2 weeks, then 30+ days.
- **Readiness checklist:** tracks the plan and supplies you actually have, not just what you know.
- **Household briefing:** a one-page plan and a short script to brief your family.
- **Daily scenario:** one short scenario a day after you finish, to keep it fresh.

## Disclaimer

This course is educational. It does not replace certified hands-on training (CPR, bleeding control, and similar) or professional medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
